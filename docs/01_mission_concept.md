# DAWN Mission Concept

## 1. Mission Statement

DAWN is a personal aerospace engineering project aimed at developing
and validating a resilient autonomous multirotor UAV.

The system will progressively integrate flight dynamics, Guidance,
Navigation and Control (GNC), sensor fusion, autonomous decision-making,
computer vision and fault-tolerant operation.

The final objective is to demonstrate the autonomous execution of a
complete UAV mission under both nominal and degraded operating conditions.

## 2. Mission Scenario

DAWN shall execute a simulated autonomous mission consisting of:

1. System initialization and pre-flight checks.
2. Autonomous take-off.
3. Navigation through a predefined sequence of waypoints.
4. Detection of GNSS degradation or loss.
5. Continuation of the mission using alternative navigation information.
6. Detection and identification of a designated landing zone.
7. Autonomous approach to the landing zone.
8. Precision landing.
9. Mission completion and system shutdown.

## 3. Nominal Mission

During nominal operation, the UAV shall use simulated onboard sensors
and GNSS information to estimate its state and autonomously navigate
through the mission.

The flight-control system shall maintain vehicle stability and track
commands generated by the guidance and autonomy layers.

## 4. Degraded Operation

DAWN will eventually evaluate the system response to several
off-nominal conditions, including:

- GNSS loss or degradation
- IMU bias or excessive sensor noise
- Barometer failure
- Camera loss
- Communication loss
- Motor performance degradation
- Low battery condition

The autonomous system shall detect relevant failures and determine
whether the mission can continue in a degraded mode or whether a safe
mission termination is required.

## 5. Initial System Capabilities

The project will progressively implement:

- Nonlinear 6-DOF multirotor dynamics
- Attitude and position control
- Guidance and trajectory tracking
- IMU, GNSS, magnetometer and barometer models
- State estimation and sensor fusion
- PX4 autopilot integration
- ROS 2-based autonomy
- Computer vision
- GNSS-denied navigation
- Health monitoring and fault detection
- Software-in-the-Loop validation
- Hardware-in-the-Loop validation

## 6. Development Philosophy

DAWN will be developed incrementally.

Each major capability shall be:

1. Defined through engineering requirements.
2. Implemented using appropriate modelling or software tools.
3. Tested under nominal and off-nominal conditions.
4. Verified against measurable performance criteria.
5. Documented with reproducible results.

The project will prioritize engineering understanding, traceability
and validation over simply demonstrating autonomous flight.
